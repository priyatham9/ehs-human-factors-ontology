# PIF suggester: evaluation against the hand-coded CSB incidents

Generated by `tools/evaluate_suggester.py`; do not hand-edit. Method, rules and caveats: `docs/suggester.html`.

Data: 10 investigations, 25 (investigation, PIF) gold pairs over 9 distinct PIFs. The narrative for each investigation is the concatenation of the quoted CSB passages that were used to code it.

**Read this first.** The input text is the evidence the gold labels were coded from, so recall here measures whether the rules' vocabulary reaches the words a coder already pointed at, not whether the suggester finds PIFs in an unseen narrative. Precision is measured against an incomplete gold standard (only PIFs with a quoted passage are coded), so some false positives may be real conditions nobody coded. The curated rules were written after the author had read these 25 quotes, so its numbers are resubstitution (optimistic); only the baseline was not fit to the incidents. With this n, one pair moves a PIF's precision or recall by 20 to 100 points. Nothing here is an estimate of performance on other incidents.
