03 / CHECK THE OTHER DIRECTION

            
A coverage gap can coexist with a mapping.

          

          

            “Local factor has no counterpart in a framework” and “external
            factor is not fully covered locally” are different assertions. The
            latter can remain true even when a partial mapping exists.
          

          
          

        

        

          

            
04 / A TESTABLE REPAIR

            

              Keep SKOS standard. Carry its missing context alongside it.
            

          

          

            

              
A concrete impossibility result

              

                Delete the source’s explicit
                WorkplaceVisibility → noCounterpartIn → SPAR-H
                assertion. The standard SKOS export is byte-identical. One
                source now asserts no counterpart; the other says nothing. No
                decoder of that export alone can recover which source it
                received.
              

              

                This is a tested information-loss counterexample, not a claim
                that SKOS is defective. The projection deliberately serves a
                narrower purpose.
              

            

            

              

              

                The sidecar preserves original alignment and gap resource IDs,
                their source keys and comments, and the original
                ehs:noCounterpartIn triples. Standard RDF
                reification identifies each absence statement; PROV records its
                exact source-file snapshot.
              

              

                It adds no invented negative SKOS property, no new mapping, and
                no assertion-specific citation that the source does not contain.
              

            

          

          

            The guarantee is a round trip of the scoped crosswalk records, not
            the full ontology. Factor levels, macrocognitive functions, failure
            modes, proxies and unrelated annotations remain outside this
            contract.
